Gmail Triage

A personal, single-user tool for keeping one Gmail inbox tidy.

Gmail Triage runs once a day on a server operated by its author. It looks at the sender and subject of newly received messages in the author's own Gmail account, scores each one as "keep" or "delete candidate", and sends the author a summary in a private Telegram chat.

Nothing is removed automatically. Messages are moved to Gmail's Trash only after the author explicitly approves them in that chat, and they can be restored from Trash for 30 days. The app never permanently deletes mail, never sends mail, and is not offered to anyone other than its author.

See the privacy policy for exactly what data is accessed and where it goes.
